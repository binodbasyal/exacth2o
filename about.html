Our Mission

exactH2O was developed to address a critical need in agricultural research and high-value crop production: the need for precise, reliable, and intelligent irrigation control. Traditional irrigation systems often lack the precision required for scientific research or the optimization capabilities needed to maximize crop quality and resource efficiency.

Our mission is to revolutionize irrigation management by combining advanced sensor technology with intelligent automation. We enable researchers to conduct reproducible studies with confidence and help commercial growers optimize their operations for maximum yield and quality while minimizing resource use.

Why exactH2O Matters

Water scarcity is one of agriculture's biggest challenges. At the same time, the demand for high-quality research and premium crops continues to grow. exactH2O addresses both challenges by providing the tools needed to use water more efficiently while achieving better results.

Whether you're a researcher studying plant responses to drought stress, a licensed cannabis cultivator optimizing cannabinoid production, or a greenhouse operator managing multiple crop types, exactH2O provides the precision and reliability you need to succeed.

Our Commitment to Research

We understand the unique requirements of scientific research. That's why exactH2O offers research-grade data logging, programmable protocols, and the ability to maintain exact experimental conditions across multiple treatment groups. Your research deserves the best tools available.

As we continue to grow, our focus remains on delivering innovative solutions that help our customers achieve their goals while promoting sustainable water use and advancing agricultural science.
