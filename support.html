FAQ

Soil moisture sensors should generally be installed at the root zone depth of your crop. For most annual crops and vegetables, 6–8 inches (15–20 cm) is ideal. For deeper-rooted plants or perennials, a second sensor at 12–18 inches can be beneficial. Always install sensors horizontally or at a slight angle to ensure full contact with undisturbed soil and avoid air pockets that can skew readings.
